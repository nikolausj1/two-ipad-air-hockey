Battle items

All 17 items in Battle mode, exactly as they are built in the game today: the 6 that shipped first plus the 11 added tonight. Each card has a looping sketch of the item on a top-down rink (you at the bottom), then the real numbers from the game code.

How to answer: press Keep, Maybe or Remove on each card, type any notes in the box, then press "Copy feedback for Claude" at the bottom and paste it into the chat. Your answers are saved in this browser, so you can come back to it.

How items are dealt

A box only counts when you shoot the puck through a box on the other player's side. Dribbling through a box in your own half does nothing. You hold one item at a time and fire it from your corner button.

A player who is behind on the score draws the offensive items more often: Rocket, Ghost, Shrink, Mine, Magnet, Wide goal, Mirror, Quake, Decoys and Steal. Those cards carry the tag "More when behind"; the rest are "Even odds".

01Rocket

Your next hit leaves like a missile.

What it does: firing it arms your mallet. Your next hit multiplies the puck speed by 1.6, and never sends it slower than 1800 px/s. For 1 s of flight after that hit the puck may go up to 3900 px/s instead of the normal speed cap.

In play: the simplest item to understand. Arm it, wait for a clean look at the goal, and hit. A hit that was going to be weak still leaves at 1800 px/s, so even a nudge becomes a real shot.

02Shield

Your goal mouth turns into a wall for a second.

What it does: for 1 s your goal mouth is solid. A puck that reaches it bounces off like a rail, and comes back 1.15 times faster than it arrived (still within the normal speed cap).

In play: a panic button. It is short, so it is about timing: fire it when a shot is already on its way and you are out of position. Fired too early it runs out before the puck arrives.

03Ghost

The puck vanishes from their iPad.

What it does: for 2 s the puck is invisible on their iPad. On yours it shows as a faint spectral outline, so you still know where it is. The physics do not change, only what they see. It is never rolled in pass and play, because both players share one screen.

In play: best fired just as you shoot, so the puck disappears while it is crossing the table. They have to guess from your swing where it went.

04Shrink

Their mallet drops to 60 percent.

What it does: their mallet shrinks to 60 percent of its normal size for 4 s. It is a real smaller body, so it covers less of their goal and reaches less of the puck. If you also have Giant, the two stack.

In play: shots that would clip the edge of their mallet now slip past it. Four seconds is long enough for one or two real attacks.

05Freeze

The puck stops dead, then carries on.

What it does: the puck stops dead where it is for 0.5 s, then resumes at exactly the velocity it had before, same speed and same direction.

In play: a timing trick. Fire it when they are swinging at your shot, so their mallet swipes at empty air and the puck carries on after they have committed. It can also save you by stopping a shot for half a second while you get back.

06Mine

A trap in their half that blasts the puck.

What it does: drops a mine at a random spot in their half, away from the goal crease and the mallets. It has a 1 s fuse, then stays armed for 10 s. The first time the puck touches it, the puck is blasted in a random direction at 92 percent of top speed, and the mine is gone. One shot only.

In play: a hazard they have to steer around in their own half. Because the blast direction is random, it can go anywhere, including back at you, so it is chaos rather than a sure goal.

07Magnet

The puck homes in on their goal.

What it does: for 2 s the puck steers toward the middle of their goal, moving at 700 px/s or faster. The pull is strong, so even a slow sideways puck turns and heads for the mouth. A hit by their mallet cancels it on the spot.

In play: a lazy puck becomes a threat, so they have to go and meet it instead of waiting. The counter is easy to read: get a mallet on it.

08Wide goal

Their goal mouth stretches to 1.5 times.

What it does: their goal mouth grows to 1.5 times its width for 6 s, capped at 60 percent of the rink width. Your own goal is untouched.

In play: angles that used to hit the post now count, and they have more mouth to cover with the same mallet. Six seconds is the longest of the attacking items.

09Goalie

A mini mallet guards your goal line.

What it does: for 5 s a mini mallet, 55 percent of a normal mallet, slides along your goal line tracking the puck at up to 1.4 rink widths per second. It hits the puck exactly like a mallet does. It never leaves the goal line.

In play: frees you to attack without leaving your goal empty. A hard shot to the far side can still beat it.

10Giant

Your mallet swells to 1.5 times.

What it does: your mallet grows to 1.5 times its size for 5 s. It is a real bigger body, so it covers more goal and reaches the puck from further away. It stacks with Shrink: a giant mallet that gets shrunk is 1.5 x 0.6 of normal.

In play: the easiest item to read, and it works for defence and attack alike.

11Catch

Grab the puck, carry it, then flick it.

What it does: firing it arms your mallet until your next touch. Then the puck sticks in front of your mallet for up to 1.5 s. Move your mallet faster than 900 px/s (a flick) and the puck flies off at 1.5 times your mallet speed. If you hold the full 1.5 s, it drops forward at 600 px/s.

In play: turns reacting into aiming. You choose the angle and wait for them to commit. The risk is that they get the same time to line up.

12Barrier

Three bricks across the middle of their half.

What it does: three round bricks, each half a mallet wide, appear across the middle of their half (at a quarter, half and three quarters of the width) for 6 s. The puck bounces off them like bumpers. Both players see them.

In play: every shot becomes a choice between threading a gap and banking off a brick, and the bricks also get in the way of their clearances.

13Mirror

Their screen flips left to right.

What it does: for 3 s their screen is drawn flipped left to right. Their controls are unchanged: the mallet still goes where the finger really is, only the picture is mirrored. Nothing changes on your screen. Never rolled in pass and play.

In play: a prank that makes a good player flail for a moment. The counter is to stop trusting the picture and play by feel.

14Quake

Their table shakes and their mallet drags.

What it does: for 2 s their screen shakes and their mallet moves only 60 percent of each finger move, so it lags behind the finger. Your screen does not shake.

In play: they can see exactly what is happening but cannot keep up, which gives you a short free attack window.

15Decoys

Two fake pucks, one real one.

What it does: for 3 s two fake pucks fan out from the real one on their screen, looking exactly like real pucks there. On your screen they are faint, so you know which is which. Only the real puck collides or scores. Never rolled in pass and play.

In play: they have to guess in a fraction of a second, a bluff on top of a real shot.

16Steal

Take the item they are holding.

What it does: takes the item they hold and puts it on your corner button, ready to fire. If they hold nothing, you get a fresh random item instead (never another Steal), so it is never wasted.

In play: punishes hoarding a strong item and makes every box worth contesting.

17Tiny puck

The puck shrinks for everyone.

What it does: the puck shrinks to 60 percent of its size for 5 s, for both players. It is harder to see, harder to block and slips through gaps that a normal puck would not.

In play: it helps whoever is attacking, which may be them, so it is a gamble rather than a sure win.
