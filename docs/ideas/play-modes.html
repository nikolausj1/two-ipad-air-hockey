New play modes

Fourteen ideas for new ways to play on two iPads. Every card runs a small self-playing simulation of the mode, with two bots, so you can watch how it plays instead of reading rules. The sims are a sketch of the feel, not the final physics.

For each card tap Yes, Maybe or No and add a note if you have one. When you are done, press "Copy feedback for Claude" at the bottom and paste the summary into the chat.

01Hot Potato

The puck is a bomb with a fuse. Do not be holding it when it blows.

The puck carries a 6 second fuse, shown as a ring that shrinks. Any mallet hit resets it to 6 seconds minus 0.5 seconds for every hit so far this rally, so long rallies get twitchy. When the fuse runs out the puck explodes and the player whose half it is in concedes a point. Goals still count as normal.

Watch for the stalling game: nobody wants to touch it late, but whoever leaves it alone in their own half pays for it.

02Breakout

Wall off both goals with bricks. Smash a hole, then shoot through it.

Each goal is walled by a row of 7 bricks. A puck hitting a brick breaks it and bounces. Only a shot through a hole in the row scores. Bricks come back after every goal.

Every attack has two jobs: open a gap and then thread it. Defenders can wait for the hole to open and guard it.

Inspired by ArkanoidEffort MBest with 2 iPads

03Tron Trails

Your mallet draws a light wall. The puck bounces off it.

Each mallet leaves a glowing trail for 2 seconds. The puck treats trail segments as walls and bounces off them. Sweep your mallet across the goal mouth to build a barrier, or lay a wall to bank the puck somewhere new. In this sim a bounce chips a gap in the wall so rallies keep flowing.

Defending becomes drawing. Look for the zigzag walls in front of each goal and shots that ricochet off them.

Inspired by Tron light cyclesEffort MBest with 2 iPads

04Twin Puck

Two pucks on the table, always. Score on either.

The serve drops two pucks. Both are live, both score, and the pucks also collide with each other. When one scores it respawns at the centre while the other keeps going, so there are always two in play.

Pure chaos mode. Watch for attention splitting: you cannot guard one puck and attack the other at once.

Inspired by pinball multiballEffort LBest with 2 iPads

05Moving Goals

The goal mouths slide along the end rails. Aim where it will be.

Each goal mouth glides side to side along its end rail, about 4 seconds per sweep, with the two goals out of phase. Everything else is classic.

Cheap to build and it changes every shot: lead your aim, or wait for the goal to swing into line.

Inspired by moving targets in Pong clonesEffort SBest with 2 iPads

06Gravity Well

A black hole in the middle bends every shot.

A well sits at the centre of the rink and pulls the puck toward it. Fast pucks curve and slingshot past. Slow pucks orbit, spiral in and get swallowed, which ends the rally with a new serve and no point.

Curved shots open up new angles. Watch for the slingshots and the doomed slow pucks.

Inspired by orbital physics gamesEffort MBest with 2 iPads

07Shrinking Rink

The side rails close in. The longer the rally, the tighter it gets.

Every 10 seconds of rally time the side rails move in by 10 percent, down to half width. The next goal resets them. In this sim the clock runs about three times faster so you can see it.

Sudden death feel: late in a rally there is no room to hide and the goal looks huge.

Inspired by battle royale zonesEffort MBest with 2 iPads

08Tug of War

One shared score bar. Every goal drags the line toward the loser.

There is a single bar across the seam instead of two scores. Each goal pushes the line 20 percent toward the loser's goal. Push it all the way to the end to win.

Comebacks are built in: a streak is easy to see and easy to break. Watch the bar swing.

Inspired by tug of warEffort SBest with 2 iPads

09Curling

No goals. Take turns sliding the puck onto the rings.

Players alternate sliding a puck toward the target on the far side. It scores by where it stops: 3 points in the inner ring, 2 in the middle, 1 in the outer. Best of 8 stones. Stones can knock each other out of the rings.

A calm, skill-and-patience mode for a change of pace. Watch for the takeout shots.

Inspired by curlingEffort MBest with 2 iPads

10Bank Shots

A goal only counts if the puck kissed a side rail first.

The puck must touch a side rail after the last mallet hit and before it enters the goal. A straight shot bounces back out of the mouth. The goal mouths glow green when the puck is armed.

Turns the table into a pool table. Watch the straight shot get rejected and the banked one go in.

Inspired by pool bank shotsEffort SBest with 2 iPads

11Blackout Rink

The lights are off. The puck is your lantern.

The rink goes dark. The puck lights a radius around itself and each mallet has a faint glow. You track the puck by its light and guess the rest.

A pure view change, no new physics. Watch how little you can see of the other half.

Inspired by lantern and fog of war gamesEffort SBest with 2 iPads

12Wind

A steady wind shoves the puck. It turns every 8 seconds.

A wind pushes the puck constantly. Every 8 seconds the direction changes, shown by an arrow in the corner and a gust sound. Fight it or ride it.

The sim shows the arrow and drifting streaks but no sound. Watch the puck creep when nobody touches it.

Inspired by wind in mini golfEffort SBest with 2 iPads

13Pinball

Four bumpers score for whoever hit the puck last.

Four bumpers sit on the rink. Each time the puck hits one, the player who last touched the puck gets 1 point. A goal is worth 5. First to 21 wins.

Rewards messy, high-bounce play. Watch the plus ones pop and the lead swing.

Inspired by pinballEffort MBest with 2 iPads

14Turbo

Every hit makes the puck 10 percent faster, with no cap.

The puck speeds up 10 percent each time a mallet hits it in a rally, until someone scores. Then it resets to normal speed.

Rallies escalate until someone cannot keep up. Watch the speed meter climb and the trail stretch.
