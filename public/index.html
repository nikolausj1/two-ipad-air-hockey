    How to play

    1. Line up. Lay two iPads flat in portrait, top edge against top edge. One iPad hosts, the other joins with the 4-letter code or QR.
    2. Get ready. The host can nudge the seam gap until the ring lines up across both screens. Then each player presses down on their own half.
    3. Play. Slide your mallet, hit the puck across the gap, and be first to the target score. One iPad works too: bot or pass & play.
